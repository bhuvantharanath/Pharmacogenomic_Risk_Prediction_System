## How these were found

Every string this project shows a user — 379 of them, 48,762 characters — was tagged with spaCy `en_core_web_sm`. Tokens tagged NOUN or PROPN were lemmatised, and a lemma is a candidate when it occurs less than once per 10,000 words in ordinary English (Zipf < 4.0).

No rule decides whether a word is biomedical, and no model judges its
meaning. The extractor only knows what is rare. Everything rare goes to
a person. The rule was recorded in `glossary_precommitment.md` before
it was run.
