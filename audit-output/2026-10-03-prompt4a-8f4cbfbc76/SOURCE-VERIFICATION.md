## azathioprine

[Maillard M et al. CPIC thiopurine guideline: 2025 update. PMID 41618934; DOI 10.1002/cpt.70209.](https://files.cpicpgx.org/data/guideline/publication/thiopurines/2026/41618934.pdf)

2025 update; epub 2026-01-31; April 2026 issue, 119(4):916–927. Official advance PDF accepted 2026-01-08.

Candidate source location: PDF page 2, TPMT and NUDT15 background; Figure 1; azathioprine recommendation table; page 11 adverse-reaction caveat.

> TPMT catabolizes mercaptopurine to an inactive methylmercaptopurine base

TPMT and NUDT15 act at different metabolic steps. The updated guidance distinguishes combined phenotypes. Some thiopurine adverse reactions are unrelated to deficiency in either gene; a normal genotype cannot establish general safety.

**Review flag:** Stored citation says Relling MV et al.; primary publication identifies Maillard M as first author. Preserve stored bytes; correct only through an authorized, subsequently reviewed source revision. The single stored gene/phenotype does not record a full combined genotype.

## clopidogrel

[Lee CR et al. CPIC CYP2C19/clopidogrel guideline: 2022 update. PMID 35034351; DOI 10.1002/cpt.2526.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9287492/)

2022 update; final edited form 2022-02-08; November 2022 issue, 112(5):959–967.

Candidate source location: Abstract; Drug: Clopidogrel / Background; Therapeutic Recommendations; Tables 2 and 3 (cardiovascular versus neurovascular indications).

> CYP2C19 catalyzes the bioactivation of the antiplatelet prodrug clopidogrel

Metabolic activation is supported, but prescribing recommendations depend on indication and contraindications. A normal/rapid result does not guarantee effectiveness or absence of bleeding. Increased active metabolite formation should not be generalized to greater clinical benefit.

**Review flag:** Stored explanations omit indication in several action statements; the entry schema has no indication field. Check each candidate pinned row population and qualifications.

## fluorouracil

[Amstutz U et al. CPIC DPYD/fluoropyrimidine guideline: 2017 update. PMID 29152729; DOI 10.1002/cpt.911.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5760397/)

2017 update; final edited form 2017-11-20; February 2018 issue, 103(2):210–216. See separate 2018 update and 2026 pending-update records.

Candidate source location: Drugs: Fluoropyrimidines / Background, paragraphs on DPD catabolism; Table 2; Potential Benefits and Risks.

> Reduced activity of DPD results in reduced clearance

The clearance-to-exposure direction is supported. Normal metabolizer guidance does not establish freedom from adverse effects. The original Table 2 alone is insufficient to verify later intermediate-metabolizer recommendations.

**Review flag:** Use pinned recommendation text plus the official update history. A pending publication is an advisory, not authority to hand-edit the biological definitions.

## simvastatin

[Cooper-DeHoff RM et al. CPIC statin-associated musculoskeletal symptoms guideline. PMID 35152405; DOI 10.1002/cpt.2557.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9035072/)

2022; final edited form 2022-03-11; May 2022 issue, 111(5):1007–1021.

Candidate source location: Abstract; Genes / SLCO1B1 background; simvastatin recommendations by SLCO1B1 function; Figure 1.

> SLCO1B1 encodes a transporter

OATP1B1 mediates hepatic uptake. Function categories and increased systemic exposure should not be rewritten as enzyme metabolizer status or guaranteed muscle injury. Normal function is compatible with residual musculoskeletal risk.

**Review flag:** Review normal-function language for the difference between assessed function and absence of all relevant genetic variation.

## codeine

[Crews KR et al. CPIC CYP2D6, OPRM1 and COMT / select opioid guideline. PMID 33387367; DOI 10.1002/cpt.2149.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8249478/)

Candidate source location: Drug: Opioids / Background; Linking Genetic Variability / Codeine; Table 2, indeterminate row.

> formation of morphine from codeine is well defined.

The activation mechanism is supported. It does not establish that this application calls CYP2D6 or can provide codeine guidance. The capability registry explicitly abstains.

**Review flag:** Keep this stored Unknown entry in the all-entry review set; make the implementation limitation explicit.

## warfarin

[Johnson JA et al. CPIC pharmacogenetics-guided warfarin dosing: 2017 update. PMID 28198005.](https://files.cpicpgx.org/data/guideline/publication/warfarin/2017/28198005.pdf)

2017 update; September 2017 issue, volume 102(3).

Candidate source location: PDF page 2, CYP2C9 and warfarin and Figure 1; prescribing recommendations and algorithm context.

> is the primary metabolizing enzyme of S-warfarin

CYP2C9 is especially relevant to S-warfarin clearance. Genotype-informed dosing requires additional genes and clinical inputs; this application has no validated dosing implementation.

**Review flag:** Avoid implying that another upload alone supplies a warfarin dosing recommendation. Retain explicit abstention and all-entry review.
